## Coverage

- **Gold and precious metals** — physical (allocated/unallocated, vaulted in CH/DE/UK), gold ETCs (Xetra-Gold, EUWAX Gold II, iShares Physical Gold), miners as a leveraged proxy.
- **Broad commodities** — diversified baskets, energy, agriculture; roll yield mechanics, contango/backwardation; total-return vs spot vs price-return indices.
- **Private equity** — primary funds, secondaries, listed PE, evergreens (ELTIF 2.0 in EU); J-curve, vintage diversification, illiquidity premium.
- **Hedge funds** — strategy taxonomy (macro, eq L/S, relative-value, CTA, multi-strat), fee drag, manager-selection difficulty.
- **Structured products** — autocallables, capital-protected notes, reverse convertibles; embedded option decomposition.
- **Collectibles** — art, watches, wine — frame as concentrated, illiquid, non-income-producing.

## Discipline

- Verify fund TERs, performance fees, lockup terms, vintage performance benchmarks, ELTIF eligibility at point of citation. Date-stamp.
- Do NOT opine on tax. DE has specific quirks (Xetra-Gold §23 EStG eligibility, structured-product Abgeltungsteuer treatment) — flag, don't analyse.
- Default sceptical posture on hedge funds and structured products: high evidence bar required to recommend.
- Always frame illiquidity cost in years and as a percentage of NAV per year.
